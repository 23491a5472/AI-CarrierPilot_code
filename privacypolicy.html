Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AI CareerPilot collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application and related services.

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

1. Introduction

AI CareerPilot ("AI CareerPilot", "we", "our", or "us") is an AI-powered career assistance application designed to help users analyze resumes, evaluate ATS compatibility, match resumes with job descriptions, prepare for interviews, and improve their career application process.

This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, stored, processed, and protected when you use the AI CareerPilot mobile application and related services.

By using AI CareerPilot, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, AI CareerPilot may collect or process the following categories of information:

Account Information

  • Email address used for account authentication
  • Account identifiers
  • Basic profile information that you voluntarily provide

Career and Resume Information

  • Resume content
  • Employment and professional experience
  • Skills and qualifications
  • Education information
  • Job descriptions submitted for analysis
  • Career preferences and information voluntarily provided by you

AI Feature Inputs

When you use AI-powered features, information you enter or upload may be processed to generate the requested analysis, recommendations, feedback, or other results.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate AI CareerPilot
  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Analyze resumes and job descriptions
  • Generate ATS compatibility insights
  • Provide resume and career recommendations
  • Provide interview preparation and feedback
  • Save user preferences and application data
  • Improve application functionality and usability
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Display advertisements in the free version
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

4. AI Processing

AI CareerPilot uses artificial intelligence services to provide features such as resume analysis, ATS evaluation, job matching, career insights, and interview assistance.

Information submitted to AI-powered features may be transmitted through our backend infrastructure and to third-party AI service providers when necessary to provide the requested functionality.

Important: AI-generated results are intended to provide assistance and general career guidance. They do not guarantee employment, interviews, job offers, or specific hiring outcomes.

AI CareerPilot does not make employment decisions on behalf of employers.

5. Resume and Job Description Data

You may voluntarily submit resumes, job descriptions, skills, employment history, education information, and other career-related information.

This information is processed to provide the features requested by you, including resume analysis, ATS matching, career insights, and other AI-powered functionality.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information that is not necessary for the career-related feature you are using.

6. Authentication and Account Management

AI CareerPilot may use third-party authentication and backend infrastructure, including Supabase, to securely manage user accounts and application data.

Authentication information is used to identify users, protect accounts, and provide access to features and information associated with the user's account.

Advertising providers may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, advertising interactions, and information necessary to provide and measure advertising, subject to applicable law and your privacy choices.

Advertising practices may vary depending on your device, location, consent choices, and applicable privacy requirements.

AI CareerPilot may rely on third-party service providers to provide, operate, secure, and improve the application.
